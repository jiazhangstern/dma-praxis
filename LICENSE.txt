IN BRIEF

DMA Praxis is an Excel add-in and companion app for simulation and
optimization. Jiawei Zhang offers this proprietary software free of charge
for education on Mac and Windows. This edition expires on June 30, 2027.

Download DMA Praxis only from its official download page,
https://github.com/jiazhangstern/dma-praxis. You may share the link to
that page with anyone. Do not share the package files themselves: do not
post, upload, email, or pass on the installers or zip files.

Students, teaching assistants, and instructors may use DMA Praxis for
coursework, self-study, teaching, and evaluation, including courses taught
by paid instructors at schools that charge tuition.

DMA Praxis stores model settings in your workbook. Depending on the
command you run, it can update cells or add worksheets. Its own macros run
from the installed Excel add-in. DMA Praxis runs analyses on your computer
without uploading your workbooks, model data, or results to an internet
service.

DMA Praxis comes without a warranty to the extent permitted by law. You
are responsible for your models and for independently checking important
results before relying on them.

On Windows, quit Excel and run Remove DMA Praxis.cmd from the extracted
installer folder. On Mac, open the DMA Praxis tab in Excel, choose Help,
and click Uninstall DMA Praxis… at the end of the Help page. Uninstalling
leaves saved workbooks and their saved model settings in place. PX formulas
need the add-in to recalculate.

Write to Jiawei Zhang at jz31@stern.nyu.edu for questions or written
permission requests.
